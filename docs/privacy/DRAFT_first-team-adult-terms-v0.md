## 1. Who provides the service

Team Manager is a team and club management application. The contracting operator and accountable legal entity must be confirmed before publication: **[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME, ABN, POSTAL ADDRESS, SUPPORT EMAIL REQUIRED]**. The organisation with responsibility for each team's data, and the roles of the platform operator, club and association, must be agreed and disclosed separately.

## 2. Initial permitted users

The first connected trial is limited to **individually verified and approved adult accounts** for St George Oztag, Mixed 20, season 2027. A person signing in, holding a Platform Owner grant or receiving an invitation does not automatically obtain permission to access any team records. Each role is approved separately and limited to the selected team. Under-18 and guardian account activation is **not enabled** in this trial.

The real team may include under-18 participants, but this trial is **not authorisation to collect or process their personal information**.

## 3. Intended service

Subject to permissions and additional approvals, the application supports team operations such as scheduling, attendance, availability, volunteer duties, team notices, and coaching. Future functionality may require additional terms or safeguarding decisions. The historic encrypted demonstration is separate from the connected first-team service.

Team Manager does not establish sporting association registration, eligibility, payments, insurance, qualifications, or child-safety clearance.

## 4. Account responsibilities

Each person must use their own account, keep their credentials private, avoid sharing sensitive or unnecessary personal information, and report suspected unauthorised access to **[CONTACT TO CONFIRM]**. Authorised administrators may review and revoke team role grants. Scope restrictions apply even when an account is signed in.

## 5. Permitted conduct

Users must access only information authorised for their specific role, club and team. They must not use the service to harass others, upload unlawful content, disclose unrelated private records, bypass controls or use information for unapproved marketing. Volunteer and safeguarding activities require the relevant independent checks, not merely a role label.

## 6. Availability, corrections and service changes

During this limited trial, service availability and recovery terms require separate operational approval: **[TRIAL SUPPORT, INCIDENT PROCESS, SERVICE LIMITATIONS, RETENTION TO CONFIRM]**. The operator must publish a method to request data corrections, role review and account closure. Material changes to the terms require a new reviewed version and fresh acknowledgement where required.
